Questions people ask

What does “verified” mean?

The Verified badge appears only after our team checks a business’s ID and registration, plus the professional licence for lawyers, dentists and agents. Businesses pay a subscription that covers this review, and approval is never guaranteed. “Contact confirmed” means only that their phone number was confirmed.

Is it free to use?

Searching, comparing and reading messages are free. Sending messages, job applications and quote requests needs a paid plan.

How do I list my business?

Choose “List your business”, fill in your details and your profile goes live straight away. Verification follows.

How are reviews kept honest?

Every review carries a name, and any that look fake or abusive are removed.
